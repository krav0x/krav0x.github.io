AfonsoAuto

Private, mobile-first stock and sales manager for a Portuguese used-car dealership. One auth-gated dashboard covers each car's full lifecycle: acquisition, reconditioning expenses, sale, and VAT settlement under the correct Portuguese regime (Regime da Margem or Regime Geral). On sale, a plaintext summary of acquisition, expenses, sale, VAT, and net result is emailed to the accountant automatically. European Portuguese UI, built to be used on a phone while walking the lot.

01 · Context

Context

Small Portuguese used-car dealerships (stands) run on paper folders, WhatsApp photos, and Excel. Every vehicle carries a growing pile of costs (mechanic, painting, IPO inspection, ISV legalisation, tyres, detailing, transport) and each sale has to be booked under one of two very different VAT regimes: Regime da Margem (VAT on the margin only, common for cars bought from private sellers) or Regime Geral (VAT on the full sale price). Choose the wrong regime or misplace an expense receipt and you either pay tax you didn't owe or miss tax you did. The dealer wanted one place to see stock, running cost per car, days in stock, and a live per-vehicle P&L that already applied the correct regime.

02 · Approach

Approach

One private, auth-gated app that owns the full lifecycle of a car from intake to sale:

  • Painel. Four live KPIs (cars in stock, immobilised capital, average margin, monthly sales) and a sortable stock table with per-vehicle expense totals.
  • Vehicle intake. Full record: plate, VIN, brand and model and version, mileage, fuel, purchase date and price, supplier type (particular, stand, auction, company), NIF, VAT regime, and the scanned purchase document.
  • Expenses tracker. Typed entries (mechanical, bodywork, tyres, IPO, ISV, transport, commission, detailing) with a deductible-VAT flag and a document upload, each attached to a specific vehicle.
  • Sale registration and apuramento. Records the sale and instantly computes margem tributável, IVA apurado at 23%, total costs, days in stock, and the net result. Green or red on the bottom line.
  • Automated sale email. On sale creation a plaintext summary (acquisition, all expenses, sale, VAT, result) is sent to the office inbox via Resend and the sale is stamped as email_enviado.

The UI is entirely in European Portuguese (Painel, Viaturas, Despesas, Apuramento, Fornecedor, Matrícula) and mobile-first. The dealer opens it on a phone while walking the lot.

03 · Engineering notes

Engineering notes

  • One pure tax function. calcularApuramento() is a small, tested pure function that implements both Portuguese regimes correctly. It's called identically from the dashboard aggregate, the vehicle detail view, and the sale API route. The regime logic exists in exactly one place.
  • Server-first rendering. Dashboard and vehicle detail pages are async React Server Components fetching directly from Supabase, so the initial paint already has data.
  • Split Supabase clients. A browser client for the login form, a server client for RSC data fetching, and a middleware client for session refresh, matching the SSR patterns of the newer @supabase/ssr SDK.
  • Fail-open on email. A Resend outage never blocks a sale. The sale is committed first, the email is attempted, and only a successful send updates the email_enviado flag.
  • Typed domain with label maps. src/types/database.ts mirrors the Postgres schema and centralises the human-readable labels for states, supplier types, and expense categories, so display strings live in one file.

Stack
